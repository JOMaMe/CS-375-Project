Family Guys
Haig Shamlian (hks55@drexel.edu), Jose Manuel Mena (jmm929@drexel.edu)
Multiplayer turn-based dungeon-crawler hidden-role game
Github: https://github.com/JOMaMe/CS-375-Project

Deployment
- Users can create games, share room code with friends to join, need exactly 4 players

Game design
- Players are assigned a random class, each with unique abilities
- One player randomly selected to be imposter, role is hidden from other players
- Players explore dungeon which has rooms of different types; navigator role passes between players; imposter can see entire map, other players cannot
- Some rooms have turn-based battles against enemies
- Players gain loot/XP as they traverse dungeon
- Final room features battle against imposter
